## Why Do Teeth Stain?

Teeth stain due to coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco, and simply ageing. The good news? Modern whitening treatments can restore your smile to its natural brilliance.

## In-Office Laser Whitening

- **How it works:** A high-concentration peroxide gel is applied to your teeth and activated with an LED or laser light.
- **Time:** 45–60 minutes in the chair.
- **Results:** Up to 8 shades brighter in a single session.
- **Who it's for:** Those wanting fast, dramatic results for a special occasion.

## At-Home Custom Tray Whitening

- **How it works:** Custom-fitted trays with professional-grade gel worn daily for 1–2 hours.
- **Time:** 2–3 weeks to see full results.
- **Results:** 4–6 shades improvement.
- **Who it's for:** Those who prefer gradual improvement at home.

## Which is Better?

Both work well, but **in-office whitening gives faster and more dramatic results**. For maintenance, we often recommend pairing it with an at-home kit.

## Is Whitening Safe?

Yes — when done by a qualified dentist. Over-the-counter kits can cause sensitivity or even enamel damage if misused. At Infinity Dental, we always evaluate your enamel health before recommending a whitening plan.
