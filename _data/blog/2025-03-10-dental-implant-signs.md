## What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone to act as an artificial root. It provides a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture — creating a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions like the real thing.

## 5 Signs You Should Consider an Implant

### 1. You Have a Missing Tooth (or Several)
The most obvious sign. Every missing tooth causes bone loss in the jaw over time. Implants are the only restoration that actively prevents this.

### 2. Your Denture Doesn't Fit Well
Loose, slipping dentures affect your ability to eat and speak. Implant-supported dentures offer rock-solid retention with no adhesives.

### 3. You Have a Severely Cracked or Broken Tooth
Some teeth are damaged beyond repair. An implant is a better long-term solution than repeated attempts to save a compromised tooth.

### 4. Your Face Looks Sunken in the Cheek Area
Without tooth roots, the jaw bone deteriorates — causing a sunken appearance. Implants stimulate the bone and preserve your facial structure.

### 5. You Are Self-Conscious About Your Smile
Quality of life matters. If missing or damaged teeth are holding you back socially or professionally, it's time to act.

## Why Act Fast?

The longer you wait, the more bone you lose — and the more complex (and costly) the implant procedure becomes. Early intervention leads to better outcomes.
