## Congratulations on Your New Implant!

You've made an excellent decision. With the right care, a dental implant can last 20–30 years or even a lifetime — outperforming every other tooth replacement option.

## The First 72 Hours After Surgery

- Avoid hard, crunchy, or hot foods
- Apply an ice pack to reduce swelling (20 min on / 20 min off)
- Do not rinse forcefully or spit — let your mouth heal
- Take prescribed medications as directed

## Daily Care Routine

1. **Brush twice daily** with a soft-bristle brush around the implant crown.
2. **Floss daily** using implant-specific floss or a water flosser.
3. **Use a non-abrasive toothpaste** — avoid whitening pastes with harsh abrasives.
4. **Rinse with antimicrobial mouthwash** to keep the gum tissue healthy.

## What to Avoid

- Smoking (significantly reduces implant success rate)
- Chewing on ice or hard objects
- Excessive alcohol consumption during healing
